# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: recruiters and agency leadership hiring for senior digital strategy
and technology roles, scanning in under a minute for proof of depth and
recency. Secondary: speaking and AI-education bookers deciding whether to put
Gabriel on a stage or in front of an organization.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio site for Gabriel René Rodríguez-Rovira. Its job is to make
the depth, range, and 25-year trajectory of his work legible fast: from a
family restaurant kitchen in Puerto Rico to a Cannes Gold Lion, spanning
campaigns, digital products, talks, and AI experiments. Success is a recruiter
or booker reaching out by email.

## Positioning

The one person who has actually done all of it at once — art direction,
digital strategy, political campaigns, agency founding, award-winning creative
technology, and hands-on AI building — and can prove each claim with a dated
entry.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: email gabriel@gaborene.com (leadership roles and speaking).
- Secondary: LinkedIn profile; browsing deeper into case studies.
- The line a visitor remembers: from a kitchen in Puerto Rico to a Cannes
  Gold Lion, always seventeen things at once.
- Belief ladder: (1) the recent work is world-class (Cannes Gold, One Show);
  (2) the range is real — strategy, technology, production, AI, teaching;
  (3) the trajectory is long and self-made; (4) he is active right now —
  talks, experiments, AI Council.
- Proof on hand: award list per case study in `data/work.ts`, press list in
  `data/press.ts`, speaking engagements in `data/identities.ts`, career
  narrative in `data/timeline.ts`, YouTube case films.

## Brand Personality

First-person, direct, specific, a little dry. No marketing fluff, no
superlatives. Credibility through plainness — the record speaks, the voice
undersells. Typographic quotes, English copy.

## Anti-references

- Marketed portfolio sites: hero taglines, buzzword clouds, "visionary"
  self-description.
- Agency-reel sites where motion hides thin content.
- Anything that reads templated; the reference pole is the personal record
  site (karpathy.ai) — density, chronology, honesty.

## Design Principles

- The record is the argument: show dated entries, not claims.
- Density over curation: volume and adjacency (a Gold Lion next to a weekend
  experiment next to a university panel) prove range structurally.
- Chronology carries the story: scrolling should feel like rewinding a life.
- Preserve the existing identity: stone palette, Datatype + PP Neue York,
  subtle motion, both themes, WCAG AA.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1 body text) in both themes. `prefers-reduced-motion`
respected globally via MotionConfig. Semantic HTML, real alt text.
